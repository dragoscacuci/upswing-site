Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Upswing has no account and no server. Your data is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups of the iPhone.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app deletes its data, unless you restore the iPhone from a backup made before. Updating the app keeps everything.

How do I add the check-in widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap +, search for Upswing, and add the medium Check-in widget. Tap a mood or energy level to log it straight away.

The rest timer doesn't show on my Lock Screen.

Check that Live Activities are allowed: Settings → Upswing → Live Activities. Notifications must also be allowed for the "Rest over" alert.

How do I time a plank?

In a workout, tap + and pick Plank or Side Plank (or any exercise measured as a Timed hold), then tap Start. You get a 3-2-1 countdown and a buzz when you reach your target.
