Privacy Policy

Upswing for iPhone · Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: Upswing does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What Upswing stores

To work, Upswing saves the following only on your device:

  • Workouts, exercises and the sets you log (weights, reps, durations, distances, steps)
  • Photos and videos you attach to exercises
  • Body weight entries
  • Mood and energy check-ins and any notes you add
  • Your settings, such as accent color and rest time

This data is shared between the Upswing app and its own home-screen widget on the same iPhone. It is not sent anywhere else.

What Upswing does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No servers: the app's developer never receives your data
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs
  • No access to Apple Health

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only when you choose to take a photo or video of a gym machine. Recordings are saved inside the app.
  • Photo library: uses the system photo picker; Upswing only receives the item you pick.
  • Notifications: only to tell you when your rest between sets is over.

Backups and deletion

Your Upswing data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, which are managed by Apple under your account. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. You can also delete individual entries inside the app.

Children

Upswing does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Upswing is a training log. Mood and energy check-ins are for personal reflection and are not intended to diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
